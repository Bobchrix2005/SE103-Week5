What * meant in my pattern:
The * wildcard matched any characters after the word data and before .txt. Therefore, data*.txt matched data1.txt, data2.txt, and data3.txt.

Why I listed matches before copying:
I listed the matching files first so I could verify exactly which files the wildcard would select. This reduces the chance of copying unintended files.

Why wildcards are dangerous with delete commands:
A wildcard can match many files at once, so using it with a delete command can remove more files than intended. I should always inspect the matches before performing a destructive operation. I should be especially careful with commands such as rm.
